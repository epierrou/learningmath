III · The integral

Area, by brute patience

How much area lies beneath y = x² between 0 and 1? No formula for a curved region, so approximate it with rectangles, then use more and thinner ones.

You've reached the canyon floor

You have met both halves of calculus. Zooming in reveals the derivative: the slope of x² is 2x. Slicing thinly reveals the integral: the area under x² from 0 to 1 is ⅓.

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus says these two ideas undo each other. That's a story for a future stop.
