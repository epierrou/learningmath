Step 1 of 3 · The big idea

An equation is a balanced scale

The purple box weighs some unknown amount. We call it x. Each teal block weighs 1. The scale is level, so both sides weigh the same: that's what "=" means.

Goal: get the box alone on one side without tipping the scale. Then you'll know exactly what x weighs.

Step 2 of 3 · Two moves

Now there are two boxes

Removing blocks gets you part of the way. But two boxes balancing eight blocks tells you something else: one box must balance half of them.

The golden rule: whatever you do to one side, do to the other. Every algebra trick you'll ever learn is that rule in disguise.

Step 3 of 3 · Your turn

Solve it in your head

No scale this time. Picture it: first take away the loose blocks, then split the rest evenly between the boxes.
